## Position and boundary

The site should help owners, landscape designers, artists, contractors, and buyers brief, design, procure, install, and maintain exterior relief and sculptural landscape work. It does not provide structural/geotechnical design, pool waterproofing, electrical design, cultural authorization, or unsupported material/performance claims.
